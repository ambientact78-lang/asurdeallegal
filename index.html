This Privacy Policy explains how Blesena collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services.

What Blesena Does

Blesena connects customers in Connecticut with independent mechanics for roadside assistance and pre-purchase vehicle inspections.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number, and selected role.
  • Request details such as service type, vehicle make/model/year, VIN, notes, seller details, and destination.
  • Customer Service Agreement signature, acceptance status, date, and agreement version.

Location Information

  • Precise location may be collected when you grant permission so Blesena can route roadside assistance and coordinate inspection arrival details.

Notification Data

  • If you opt in, we may collect a push notification token so we can send job updates, ETA updates, inspection scheduling notices, and status changes.

Payment Information

  • Payments may be processed by a third-party payment processor. Blesena does not intend to store full payment card numbers on its own servers.

How We Use Information

  • To create, display, dispatch, and manage service requests.
  • To connect customers and mechanics and coordinate job status.
  • To process payments, refunds, cancellations, and disputes.
  • To enforce the Customer Service Agreement and off-platform contact policy.
  • To provide customer support and improve reliability.

How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate Blesena, including:

  • With mechanics: request details necessary to complete a job, such as service type, vehicle details, notes, and destination/location.
  • With payment processors: information needed to process payment and refunds.
  • For legal or safety reasons: when required by law or to protect users, mechanics, or Blesena.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We keep records for as long as needed to operate Blesena, handle disputes, comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, and improve platform reliability.

Your Choices

  • Location: You can disable location permissions in device settings, but some features may not work.
  • Notifications: You can opt out in device notification settings.
  • Access or deletion: You may contact us to request help with your data.

Children's Privacy

Blesena is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date when we do.
